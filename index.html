Privacy Policy

Shining Star High School App

Developed and operated by Syed's Solutions

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Syed's Solutions ("we", "us", "our") develops and operates the Shining Star High School mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. Your privacy matters to us, and the App has been designed to work without collecting anything from you.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, process, or share any personal information or user data. Specifically:

  • The App does not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect email addresses, usernames, or passwords.
  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, addresses, or any other personal details.
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

3. How the App Works

When you open the App, you can view the list of classes. When you select a class, you can read information published by the school for that class, such as homework and announcements. This content is provided for viewing only. You do not submit, upload, or enter any information of your own.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because we do not collect any user data, we have nothing to sell, rent, trade, or share with third parties. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

5. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored about you, there is no user data that could be exposed through the App. We take reasonable care to keep the App and the class content it displays accurate and secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App may be used by students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no information, no personal data from children is gathered, stored, or shared.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
